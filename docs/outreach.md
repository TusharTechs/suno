# Outreach drafts

Lead with the smallest ask: a 20-minute teacher chat with no recording. The bigger ask, recording lessons, comes second, and the safeguards are stated up front. Fill in `[dates]`. For the teacher sessions, 20–30 Oct still lands in time for the Build deck.

**Where to send:**
- programme teams at Room to Read, the Language and Learning Foundation, Pratham, Akanksha and Bharti Foundation
- low-fee or NGO-run schools you know personally

Government schools are slower: in Rajasthan and UP, a principal's written permission is mandatory.

## 1. Email to NGOs
**Subject:** Request: help testing a Grade 1–3 reading tool with teachers (Top 30, AI for Foundational Learning Hackathon)

Dear [Name],

SUNO listens to an ordinary Grade 1–3 reading period through the teacher's phone. There are no tests and no extra teacher time. 90-second concept video: youtu.be/e8ZQBmMNkPY

We are two engineers, and we need to test SUNO with real teachers before our prototype deadline on 1 November and the finale on 18 November. Could [organisation] help with any of these?

How we protect children and teachers: audio only, first names only, and the teacher starts and stops the recording. SUNO never scores the teacher. Audio is deleted after transcription, and results are shared only with the school.

Even 15 minutes to point us to the right person would help enormously.

Thank you,
Tushar and Saikiran, Team Sunvai

## 5. Question in the Top 30 WhatsApp group: sent and answered
```text
Hi everyone, Tushar from Team Sunvai (SUNO, Challenge 2). Two quick questions for the organisers that may help other teams too:

1. For the Build stage, what counts as "proof of testing and refinement basis user input"? Would teachers' feedback on our prototype's outputs count, or do you expect testing in a real classroom?
2. The AMA said synthetic data is fine if clearly labelled. Does that also cover classroom audio for the demo, if real recordings aren't possible before 1 Nov?

Thank you!
```

**The organisers' reply (around 16 Oct):**
- **Teachers, students, parents:** teams score favourably if they actually reached out and watched these users interact with the solution.
- **Niche users (DEOs, BEOs, CRPs):** where real input isn't available, they want the domain mentors' inputs and how those shaped the product.
- **Data:** the closest proxy is acceptable, such as testing with 4–5 children of the same age group. Showing it working in a real setting scores higher.
- **Scoring:** it accounts for what data and input was realistically available.
