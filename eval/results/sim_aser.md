# A 12-week term in ASER-calibrated classrooms (SIMULATED children, PUBLISHED distributions)

Generated by `eval/sim/run_aser_sim.py`: 200 simulated classes of 35 enrolled children per state, 12 weeks, 5 children noticed a day, SUNO's real tracker and planner.

**What is real here:** each class's reading levels follow ASER 2024's published Std II table for the state, and absence follows the state's published attendance in government primary schools (Pratham / ASER Centre, `eval/sim/aser2024.json`).

**What is not:** the children, the teacher and her notes are simulated. Two assumptions: ASER's single "word" level is split half simple words, half words with vowel signs; each child's absence rate is spread around the state average (Beta, concentration 4). ASER's table covers government and private schools together; government-school children read lower. Per-statement error rates: eval/results/sim_pipeline.json (Stage A, after one-tap answers; error rates floored at 3/123).
